# PC Remote promotion material

PC Remote turns an Android or iPhone browser into a local remote for Windows and macOS. Download, run, scan the QR code, and control volume, media and apps over the same Wi-Fi/LAN.

- Website: https://mimaxon1.github.io/pc-remote/
- Setup: https://mimaxon1.github.io/pc-remote/guide/
- Volume guide: https://mimaxon1.github.io/pc-remote/control-pc-volume-from-phone/
- Comparison: https://mimaxon1.github.io/pc-remote/unified-remote-alternative/
- AlternativeTo submission copy: [ALTERNATIVETO.md](ALTERNATIVETO.md)
- Screenshots and animation: [media/](media/)

The screenshots show the actual interface with sample responses. The animation demonstrates the interface, not physical PC control. Do not describe this as screen sharing, a remote mouse/keyboard, or Bluetooth control. On macOS, media controls target Music.

## Search visibility

The buildless site lives in `site/`, with English and Russian pages, unique titles/descriptions, canonical and language links, crawlable navigation and `sitemap.xml`. `.github/workflows/pages.yml` publishes it on main updates. No paid hosting, domain or analytics is required.

After publication, verify the site in Google Search Console and submit `https://mimaxon1.github.io/pc-remote/sitemap.xml`. Verification requires the site owner's Google account. Publication and sitemap availability do not guarantee indexing or rankings.

## Visual direction and verification

Dark background #111318, emerald #34d399, system typography, one real controller preview, two primary actions, three setup steps and two useful guides. Desktop two-column layout becomes one column on phones. Compared with the concept: real controller capture replaces the illustrative mockup; logo uses the new monitor/phone icon; copy, palette, section order and actions are retained. The preview frame uses a top-aligned crop to keep the first screen compact; full captures are in the README.
