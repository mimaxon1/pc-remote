Connect your phone to PC Remote

Download, scan a QR code, and open your remote.

What you need

Windows 10/11 or macOS 12+, a phone with a modern browser, and the same Wi-Fi/LAN. No mobile app is required.

Connect your phone

  1. Download the Windows installer or portable ZIP, or the universal Mac app. Run PC Remote.
  2. Choose “Show phone pairing QR code” from the Windows system tray or Mac menu bar.
  3. Connect your phone to the same network and scan the QR code with its camera.
  4. Set a PIN if prompted on first setup. Then use the volume, media and app controls.

If the phone cannot connect

Check that the phone is not on guest Wi-Fi, both devices can reach each other, and your firewall allows PC Remote on your private network. Open a fresh pairing QR code from the app menu.

Mac permissions

Media controls target the Music app. Window actions may need Automation and Accessibility permissions in macOS settings. The app is not yet notarized by Apple, so the first launch may require explicit approval in macOS.

Where it works

PC Remote is for a trusted local network. It does not stream the screen or control the mouse. For access from another network, use a secured VPN instead of exposing the app ports to the internet.
