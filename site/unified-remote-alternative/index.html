A Unified Remote alternative without a phone app

PC Remote is a free browser-based remote for Windows and macOS. Compare its features and limits with Unified Remote.

What is different?

PC Remote is a focused option for volume, media, apps and window actions on your home network. Install it on your computer, then open the controller in your phone browser.

On your phone
PC Remote uses your browser. Unified Remote offers a mobile app paired with a computer server.
Controls
PC Remote includes volume, media buttons, app launch, window actions and power controls. Unified Remote also offers mouse, keyboard, Wake-on-LAN and additional remotes; some features require its paid version.
Price and source
PC Remote is free and open source under Apache 2.0. Unified Remote has a free tier and a paid full version.
PC Remote limitations
No screen streaming, remote mouse, keyboard or Bluetooth connection. Mac media buttons target Music.

Which one fits?

For mouse, keyboard or many specialized remotes, explore Unified Remote. For volume, music and apps without installing anything on your phone, try PC Remote.
